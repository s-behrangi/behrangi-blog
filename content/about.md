I'm a hobbyist programmer finally feeding my childhood fascination with code. These days I'm learning about shaders—I take inspiration from games that achieve an austere aesthetic pleasure through shader magic, and I want to make interactive programs and games that are both visually hypnotizing and mechanically satisfying. 

Most recently, I created [a visualizer for cellular automata](https://s-behrangi.itch.io/gavazn) inspired by television sets from the 70s.

"S. Behrangi" is an alias, [deliberately chosen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samad_Behrangi). I share my country of origin with the true owner of the name, though I have been living in North America since I was eight years old. I use any pronouns, but it is phenomenologically relevant that I'm closest to what is commonly called a trans woman.
